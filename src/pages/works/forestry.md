from the astronaut, <br>
there’s a blur <br>
not in specific <br>
not in consideration

from the pilot, <br>
there’s patches of green <br>
maybe interesting <br>
maybe not

from the farmer, <br>
there’s distant lumber <br>
the bounds of their pasture <br>
the thicket of ergo sum

from the axman, <br>
there’s towers of oak <br>
making their business <br>
making their claim <br>

i am nothing, <br>
the trees litter my face
